12

Redemption

BUILT

Runs when a guest accepts a deal and the server enters the 6-character code. It re-checks every rule, then adds the reward as a separate line on the bill, takes it out of stock and records revenue and profit.

Status: Works today. 5 wrong codes lock that table’s redemption for 5 minutes.

Gets from
  • 7. Staff app enters code
Sends to
  • 17. Supabase database adds reward line

Lives in: redeem() in lib/engine.ts

13

Order intake

FIX READY

Receives every new order, from staff or the till. It ignores repeats, checks stock, takes items out of stock, saves the order and then asks the rules engine to decide.

Status: Works, with one bug: when a till re-sends the same order, it’s rejected as a conflict instead of being recognised as a repeat. Fix ready in sim/fixes.patch.

Gets from
  • 8. Order webhook order
  • 7. Staff app order
  • 17. Supabase database 1 · reads first
Sends to
  • 14. Rules engine 2 · decides

Lives in: ingestOrder() in lib/server/commands.ts

14

Rules engine

BUILT

The brain. For each new order it checks whether the table can get a deal at all, finds deals that fit the basket, removes any that break stock, safety, limits or profit rules, scores the rest, and shows the best one 78% of the time. It writes down a reason either way.

Status: Works today. Deals only look at the basket, not party size, zone or time.

Gets from
  • 13. Order intake 2 · decides
  • 15. Rulebook reads
Sends to
  • 17. Supabase database 3 · saves decision

Lives in: evaluate() and matches() in lib/engine.ts

15

Rulebook

BUILT

Everything the engine reads before deciding: the 5 deals and their prices and limits, menu costs and stock, and the venue settings (S$2.50 profit floor, score 65, 78% chance, 15% control).

Status: Works today. Changeable in the app, except new kinds of deal, which need code.

Gets from
  • starts here
Sends to
  • 14. Rules engine reads

Lives in: Offers, Menu and Settings pages · starting values in lib/seed.ts

Ideas you might add

None of these exist yet. Effort is a rough guide: small is a day or two of work, medium about a week, large several weeks.

IdeaPlugs intoEffortWhat it would take
Deals by party size, zone or timeRules engineSmall–mediumTables already store seats and zone. Add conditions in matches() and fields on each deal, e.g. “terrace only” or “Fri 5–7pm”.
Fix the bottle deal’s name checkRules engineSmallReplace the “ribeye or pasta” name match with a “pairs with wine” flag on menu items.
Deal builder (no code)Rulebook + engineMedium–largeA form on the Offers page: if basket has X and not Y, offer Z at price P. Needs new database fields and a generic matcher.
Manager alerts and daily summaryn8nSmall–mediumn8n flow on a schedule or event: low stock, repeated code guessing, end-of-night totals by WhatsApp or email.
Automatic “welcome back” rewardsMembers + engineSmall–mediumGive a member a return reward after a qualifying visit. Today it only exists in the demo data.
Real till connectionTill connectorMedium–largeWrite the adapter for your POS: map bills to visits and products to menu items, handle voids, splits and table moves.
Staff invites and roles in the appStaff appMediumToday staff accounts are added in Supabase by hand.
Member account recoveryMember portalMediumA self-service way to move an account to a new phone number.
GST and service charge in analyticsAnalyticsSmallPrices are treated as excluding tax and service charge today.
Learned recommendationsRules engineLargePick the deal most likely to add profit using visit history. Needs months of real data and control-group results first.
Multiple venuesDatabase + staff appLargeThe tables already carry a venue ID. Needs venue switching and shared member identity.

Out of scope by design: taking payments. Your till stays in charge of the bill and settlement, and TablePulse only adds a reward line.
